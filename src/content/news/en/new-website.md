Our website has a new look. Above the sea of clouds you find vast.ch, our company: together with our clients, we build research-grade software and machine-learning models for the physical world.

Flip the switch at the top of the page and the sky takes you down through the clouds, to a lake: this is vast.earth, our non-profit association. It supports open-source projects of social and environmental benefit with working hours, and brings the people behind them together. Its own pages are coming soon; meanwhile, you can [join us on Discord](https://discord.gg/PGZgkmnVf).

This page is where we will share what we work on: projects, research, and what we learn along the way. To hear from us, subscribe by email below, or follow the RSS or Atom feed.
